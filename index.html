FAQ

Good questions

What do I actually get from a sprint?
Three redesigned screens, built in your codebase and delivered as a pull request. You also get a DESIGN.md rules file and design tokens so Cursor, Lovable or Claude keep new screens consistent, plus a short Loom walking through every decision. One revision round is included.
Do you work in my actual code?
Yes. Give me access to the GitHub repo and I ship a PR against your stack, usually React, Tailwind and shadcn/ui. Lovable, Bolt and v0 projects can all sync or export to GitHub. If yours can’t, I’ll hand over the code as a zip.
Can’t I just prompt my AI to “make it look premium”?
You can, and you’ll get a different flavor of the same default. What’s missing is judgment: hierarchy, spacing, restraint, knowing what to remove. I make those calls, then write them down as rules your AI follows next time.
Who is this for?
Founders of AI-built apps that are getting real: paying users, a raise in progress, or a launch date like a Product Hunt day or demo day. If you’re still exploring an idea, the $49 Kit is the better place to start.
What if I don’t love the result?
Every sprint includes a revision round, and you see direction on day 2, before anything is built. Refund terms are confirmed in writing before you pay.
Who’s behind Vibe-to-Premium?
I’m Dhaval Patel, a product designer. It’s a one-person studio: no account managers, no handoffs. You talk to the person doing the work.
